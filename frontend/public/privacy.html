Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Local-First Data Commitment

This platform is designed as a local-first application. All data generated, processed, or uploaded through this platform, including uploaded contract drafts (.docx, .pdf), converted markdown files, metadata database tables (SQLite), and vector store files are stored strictly on your local computer's storage drive.

2. Data Transmission

We do not transmit your uploaded documents, company logs, or user credentials to any external servers, except for:

  • OpenAI API: Contract clauses and relevant policy segments are sent to OpenAI servers to retrieve vector embeddings (via text-embedding-3-large) and to perform compliance checks (via gpt-5-nano). Please consult OpenAI's privacy policy regarding data retention policies for API invocations.

3. Third-Party Services

This application does not load tracking pixels, advertising scripts, or third-party cookies. It functions as a secure utility for contract reviews.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this local application, please refer to the project's repository developed by Bhargav Rokade.
